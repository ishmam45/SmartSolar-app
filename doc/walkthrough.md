## Key Equations in the Paper

$$P_{ideal} = \eta \cdot A \cdot G \cdot [1 - \beta(T_{mod} - 25)]$$

$$P_{dc} = P_{ideal} \cdot f_{soil} \cdot f_{age} \cdot f_{shade} \cdot f_{wire} + \varepsilon$$

$$T_{mod} = T_{amb} + \frac{NOCT - 20}{800} \cdot G$$

$$t_{backup} = \frac{E_{panel} + E_{battery}}{W_{load}}$$

## How to Compile the LaTeX to PDF

### Option 1: Online (No installation needed)
1. Go to **https://texlive.net/run**
2. Clear the editor (Ctrl+A, Delete)
3. Paste the full content of `smartsolar_ieee_paper.tex`
4. Click **"TeXLive.net"** button at the bottom
5. Wait ~30 seconds for compilation
6. Click the download icon in the PDF viewer

### Option 2: Local MiKTeX / TexLive
```bash
pdflatex smartsolar_ieee_paper.tex
pdflatex smartsolar_ieee_paper.tex  # run twice for cross-references
```

### Option 3: Overleaf (requires account)
1. Create new project → Upload Files
2. Upload `smartsolar_ieee_paper.tex` and all 6 PNG figures from `outputs/` into a `figures/` folder
3. Compile

> [!NOTE]
> When compiling with figures included, you need the 6 PNG files from `outputs/` to be in a `figures/` subdirectory relative to the .tex file. Without figures, the paper still compiles but shows blank figure placeholders.
